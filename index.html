About MediLedger
Blockchain-Based Healthcare Data Interoperability Platform
🎯 Project Overview
MediLedger is a final-year engineering project that demonstrates a blockchain-based solution for healthcare data interoperability. The platform enables secure sharing of patient medical records across hospitals, labs, and pharmacies using Ethereum smart contracts and IPFS decentralised storage. Patients retain full ownership and control of their data through cryptographic consent mechanisms.
🔬 Problem Statement
Healthcare data is fragmented across isolated systems. Patients must physically carry records, leading to medical errors, delays, and privacy risks when sharing with multiple providers.
✅ Our Solution
A decentralised platform that stores encrypted records on IPFS, anchors their hashes on Ethereum, and provides role-based access for patients, doctors, and admins — all verifiable without a central authority.
👥 Team & Academic Context
This project is developed as part of a Final Year B.E. / B.Tech project in Computer Science / Information Technology. It serves as a demonstration of blockchain application in a real-world healthcare context, covering system design, smart contract development, decentralised storage, and frontend engineering.
🛡
Security Architecture
🔐 Data Integrity via Hash Generation
Every uploaded file is hashed using SHA-256 before upload. This cryptographic fingerprint is stored on the Ethereum blockchain. Any tampering with the file changes its hash — making modifications instantly detectable by anyone who re-computes the hash.
☁ Decentralised & Encrypted Storage
Records are encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM before leaving the user's device. The ciphertext is stored on IPFS — a distributed peer-to-peer network. No central server holds patient data, eliminating single points of failure.
🧑‍⚕️ Role-Based Access Control
Access is controlled by on-chain consent: patients explicitly grant or revoke access per doctor. The ConsentManager smart contract verifies wallet addresses before any retrieval is permitted. Admins have system-level visibility but cannot access encrypted file content.
🔮 Zero-Knowledge Proof Authentication
Users prove identity without exposing sensitive credentials using ZK-SNARKs. The proof demonstrates knowledge of a secret without revealing the secret itself — preserving privacy even during the authentication process.
📜 Immutable Audit Trail
Every action — upload, access, consent change — is logged as a transaction on the Ethereum blockchain. This creates a tamper-proof audit trail that supports compliance requirements under HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO 27001.
⏱ Time-Limited QR Tokens
QR codes generated for doctor access contain time-limited JWT tokens that expire after 24 hours. Each use is logged on-chain. Emergency access requires a multi-factor verification step even when scanning a valid QR code.

Benefits of Blockchain in Healthcare
🔒
Immutable Record Integrity
Once a medical record is anchored on-chain, it cannot be altered or deleted. This eliminates fraudulent modifications, ensures audit compliance, and builds trust between patients and providers.
🌐
Cross-Institution Interoperability
Blockchain enables any authorised institution — regardless of their internal EHR system — to verify and access patient records through a common, decentralised protocol. Eliminates proprietary silos.
🧑
Patient Data Sovereignty
Patients own their private keys and control who can access their data via on-chain consent contracts. Unlike centralised EHR systems, no hospital can unilaterally revoke or grant access without patient approval.
🚑
Emergency Access via QR
In emergencies, doctors scan a patient's QR code to instantly retrieve critical information — blood group, allergies, medications — without needing account credentials. The access is still logged on-chain.
📊
Transparent Compliance Audit
Every access event is permanently recorded on the blockchain. Regulatory bodies can audit compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, and other standards without relying on mutable server logs that could be tampered.
🧬
Research Data Integrity
Anonymised patient data used in research can be cryptographically verified as unmodified. Blockchain provenance proofs support reproducibility in clinical trials and population health studies.
🚀
Future Enhancements
🤖
AI-Powered Diagnostic Assistance
Integrate federated ML models trained on de-identified records to provide real-time differential diagnosis support, drug interaction warnings, and predictive risk scoring at the point of care.
📱
Mobile App (React Native)
Native iOS and Android applications enabling patients to upload records from their phones, receive push notifications for access events, and share QR codes directly from the mobile wallet.
🌐
Cross-Chain Interoperability (Polkadot / Cosmos)
Extend beyond Ethereum to enable healthcare record sharing across different blockchain networks using cross-chain messaging protocols for truly global healthcare interoperability.
🩺
Telemedicine Integration
Embed video consultation directly within the platform with automatic session recording, consent logging, and prescription generation — all anchored to blockchain records.
🧬
Genomic Data Integration
Securely store and selectively share genomic sequencing data using homomorphic encryption, enabling personalised medicine while preserving patient genetic privacy.
⌚
Wearable & IoT Data Streams
Real-time ingestion of continuous health metrics from smartwatches, glucose monitors, and bedside sensors with anomaly detection triggering automatic blockchain audit entries.
